## Xiaomi Mi Scale → Home Assistant

[![Watch the demo on YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/LJl9GLgwjTk/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/LJl9GLgwjTk)

The video shows the real setup: Xiaomi Mi Scale sends the measurement over Bluetooth Low Energy to an ESP32, which passes the data to Home Assistant through ESPHome.

> The video is provided as a practical demonstration of the project. Your hardware, Home Assistant dashboard and ESPHome configuration may look different.
